## Full description (max 4000)
```
Use AI. Keep your data.

EraseAI Firewall checks what you are about to send before you send it. If it finds an API key, password, card number, email address or phone number, it holds the message and shows you what it found. You decide: Cancel, Sanitize & Send, or Send Anyway.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose the AI apps to protect, such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.
2. Turn protection on. EraseAI only watches the apps you chose.
3. Use AI as usual. Before a message goes out, EraseAI checks it.
4. If something is found, choose Cancel, Sanitize & Send (sensitive values are replaced with labels such as [EMAIL ADDRESS]) or Send Anyway.

WHAT YOU GET
• Checks before you send: finds API keys, access tokens, private keys, database connection URLs, payment card numbers (with a checksum test), email addresses and phone numbers.
• You stay in control: every time, you see what was found and choose what happens.
• Only the apps you choose: add or remove protected apps whenever you like.
• EraseAI Keyboard (optional): another way to protect, holding risky text at the input layer.
• Manual and share scan: paste text, or share text from another app to EraseAI to check it.
• EraseAI Safe: sanitize files before you attach them (EraseAI Personal).
• Scan history: review past checks (EraseAI Personal).

FREE TRIAL
Free for 7 days or 25 messages, whichever comes first. You do not need an account to start, and without an account the checks run on your phone. To keep protection on after the trial, create an account and subscribe to EraseAI Personal.

ABOUT THE ACCESSIBILITY PERMISSION (PLEASE READ)
EraseAI Firewall uses Android's Accessibility service for one purpose: to inspect the text you are typing in the AI apps you selected, so it can warn you or sanitize it before it is sent.
• You turn this on yourself in system settings, and you can turn it off at any time.
• It only checks the apps you chose. Password fields are skipped.
• It is not used to control your phone or for any other purpose.

THIS IS NOT A VPN
EraseAI Firewall is not a VPN. It does not route or inspect your network traffic. It checks the text you type in your chosen apps before you send it.

PRIVACY
Privacy policy: https://eraseai.ai/privacy
Website: https://eraseai.ai

EraseAI is made by Vantward Solutions Pte. Ltd., Singapore.

Note: detection is rules-based (pattern matching with validation checks), not AI judgment. It can miss sensitive data it has no rule for, and it can flag harmless text. Please review before you send.
```
