## Two-minute live presentation

Use this version when the animation is looping silently or playing at low volume behind you.

“Every day, employees use generative AI to work faster. But in the process, they may paste source code, customer information, credentials, or financial data into tools the company does not control.

Traditional network firewalls were not designed to understand conversational prompts. They can see traffic, but they cannot reliably identify the sensitive meaning inside an AI interaction. That creates a growing security and compliance gap.

EraseAI closes that gap. It is an enterprise AI data firewall that inspects prompts before they leave the user’s environment. It discovers sensitive information, classifies the risk, and redacts or anonymizes what should not be shared—without preventing employees from using AI productively.

The timing matters. AI adoption is accelerating, regulation is becoming more demanding, and the financial impact of data breaches continues to rise. EraseAI sits at the intersection of the expanding AI cybersecurity and data-loss-prevention markets.

Our product-led model begins with accessible individual adoption and expands into paid team, professional, and enterprise deployments. For larger organizations, we can support controlled and on-premise environments.

We are raising a two-and-a-half-million-dollar seed round to accelerate research and development, expand enterprise sales, and secure our first major customers.

EraseAI helps organizations adopt AI without losing control of their most sensitive information. We invite you to join us in securing the AI-native future. Thank you.”

## Delivery notes

- Keep the pace measured; pause briefly after “EraseAI closes that gap.”
- Lower the music to roughly 20–25% when speaking live.
- Do not read the figures from the screen twice; let the animation carry the detailed numbers.
- End on the EraseAI closing frame, then pause before taking questions.
