## Slide 1: Pre-seed  ·  SGInnovate  ·  November 2026
**Stop the leak at the Send button.**

EraseAI Firewall checks what your people are about to send to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, on the device by default, with no proxy in the middle.


## Slide 2: The problem
**Work now happens in AI chats. The leak happens in one keystroke.**

- A log file with a live key in it. A customer list “just to summarise”. A contract, to find one clause.
- Nobody is careless. They are fast, and the chat box accepts anything.
- Once it is sent, it cannot be taken back.

## Slide 3: Why it is still happening
**Most controls see the message after it has left.**

- **Policies and training**: Rely on memory at the worst moment: a deadline, a clipboard and a chat box.
- **Network tools and gateways**: See traffic after the fact, and have to sit in the middle of it.
- **Checks at Send**: The right place. A handful of browser extensions do it. It is not yet a company-grade control.

## Slide 4: The product
**An outgoing firewall at the Send button.**

- **1  Check**: Every message, on the device by default, before it goes.
- **2  Hold**: If it finds personal data, card numbers, passwords or API keys, it stops and shows what and where.
- **3  You decide**: Cancel, Sanitize & Send, or Send Anyway. A person always has the last word.

_No proxy: nothing sits between your people and the AI.  Rules, not guesswork: patterns plus validators such as the card-number checksum. It can miss things and flag harmless text, which is why a person decides._

## Slide 5: What is live today
**Four surfaces, one decision record.**

- **Chrome extension**: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Replit. Free checks run on the device. Paid plans scan attachments in the browser.
- **Android app**: A send gate inside the AI apps you choose. Not a VPN.
- **Teams dashboard**: Invite people, see an audit log of decisions (level, action, category), never the text anyone typed.
- **Developer API**: Scan the text going into and out of your own AI application.

_Managed rollout through Chrome policy is built. Its first live deployment is a pre-seed milestone._
