# EraseAI — Investor Outreach Emails

Cold and warm outreach templates for the EraseAI seed raise ($2.5M). Pair these with
the investor list (`EraseAI_Investor_List.csv`) and the standalone investor deck.

**How to use**
- Swap every `[bracketed]` field before sending. Never send with brackets intact.
- Send from a personal address (founder), not a shared inbox. Reply-to: `director@vantward.com`.
- One firm per email. Personalize the first line (the `[why this firm]` hook) for every Tier-1 name.
- Attach the deck as a PDF, or link it — do not paste 12 slides into the body.
- Keep the first touch under ~150 words. Investors skim on mobile.
- All figures below are verified from cited public sources; the ROI/return framing is flagged as illustrative because EraseAI is pre-revenue.

## Personalization checklist (Tier-1 firms)

Before sending to a high-priority firm, fill these in:
1. **[why this firm]** — a specific portfolio company, thesis line, or recent investment that maps to EraseAI. (Pull from the "Why Fit" column of the investor list.)
2. **[First name]** — the named partner from the "Key Partner(s)" column.
3. **Channel** — email vs. submission form (see "Contact Method" / "Email Status" columns). Use Email 6 for form/generic inboxes.
4. **One concrete update** — newest pilot, shipped feature, or metric, for the follow-up.

## Sending notes
- Verify deliverability (NeverBounce/ZeroBounce) before a batch send; several list emails are PATTERN-inferred, not confirmed.
- Space follow-ups 4–5 business days apart; cap at 2 follow-ups per firm.
- Track opens/replies per firm so you can prioritize warm responders.
