## 1. Vision (30 seconds)

> **Make AI forget what it should never learn.**

Employees will keep using ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. The enterprise cannot secure what it cannot see — and legacy DLP was built for email attachments and network egress, not conversational prompts.

**EraseAI’s vision** is to become the **control plane for AI interactions**: the enforcement layer at Send that discovers sensitive data, scores risk, and acts — sanitize, block, or audit — before anything reaches an external model.

**Wedge → expansion**

| Phase | What we ship | Who pays |
|-------|----------------|----------|
| **Now** | Browser extension + Android firewall + API | Free trial / $5 Personal |
| **Next 12–18 mo** | Team admin, shared policy, audit export | **$99/mo Team** (primary revenue) |
| **Scale** | Enterprise SSO, SIEM, VPC / on-prem | Custom contracts |

We **govern use, not forbid it** — the category is **AI DLP at the interaction layer**, inside a ~24% CAGR AI-cybersecurity market (Grand View Research, per deck).

3. **GTM** — founder-led sales plus 1–2 hires to convert pilots; security-community and PLG motion.
4. **Compliance runway** — legal, privacy, path toward SOC 2 / enterprise procurement.
